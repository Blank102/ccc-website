Meet Your Instructor

Kavita —
Teaching Food with Heart

Creative Cookery Classes was founded in 2006 by Kavita with a simple belief — good food should be accessible to everyone. What started as small local classes has today grown into a trusted culinary institution, helping thousands of students across India discover their passion for cooking and baking.

With over 20 years of experience and 60+ specialised courses, Kavita teaches everything from beginner-friendly workshops to advanced diploma programmes. Her teaching style is known for being patient, practical and easy to follow, breaking down even complex techniques into simple step-by-step learning. Over the years, she has also received multiple awards and recognitions in the culinary field and has been invited to judge several cooking and baking competitions.

Every recipe taught at Creative Cookery Classes is 100% vegetarian and preservative-free. Whether you want to bake for your family, start your own baking venture, launch a cloud kitchen, or build a professional career in the culinary industry — there’s a course here designed to support your journey with confidence.
